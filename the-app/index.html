The app

Built on the boat,
for the boat.

Your spots

Your sea, mapped.

Every spot you fish, on a nautical chart with lighthouses, buoys and depth lines. Paste coordinates straight from the sounder, or drop a pin where you are.

  • Private by default, shared with the crew in one tap
  • Coordinates in DDM, DMS or decimal
  • Depth, bottom type, notes and sonar screenshot

Log a catch

Twenty seconds, gloves on.

Snap the fish, tap who caught it, pick the lure. Position, wind, swell, water temperature and moon phase are added automatically.

  • Photo and video of every catch
  • Works offline at sea, syncs back at the port
  • GPS hidden in photos is removed before upload

Your lure box

Know what really catches.

Photograph every lure once. Spartel ranks them by catches, species and spot, for you and for the whole crew.

  • Jigs, madai, stickbaits, poppers, swimbaits
  • Lure of the month for the crew
  • Best lure shown on every spot

Trips & Humminbird

From the sounder to the story.

Export your waypoints and tracks from the boat's Humminbird Solix, import them in Spartel, and every trip becomes a timeline: route, distance, speed and every fish on the map.

  • GPX import and export with the Solix
  • Crew on board, duration, distance, top speed
  • Replay any trip, catch by catch

The crew

One crew. One leaderboard.

Invite your friends with a link. See who is on the boat, who caught the biggest this month, and which spots the crew shares.

  • Invite link and crew code
  • Monthly leaderboard and records
  • Several crews if you fish with different boats
Offline at sea

The app opens without network. Catches wait on your phone and upload when you are back in range.

Private by default

Nothing leaves your account until you share it with the crew. Nothing is ever public.

Conditions logged

Wind, gusts, swell, water temperature, pressure and moon saved with every fish.
