Works with your sounder

Your Humminbird, in your pocket.

  1. 01On the Solix: My Data → Export All, to the SD card.
  2. 02In Spartel: import the GPX. Waypoints become spots, tracks become trips.
  3. 03Back the other way: export the crew’s spots and load them on any boat.
