The crew

We fish where two seas meet.

Spartel was founded in Barcelona in June 2026, by friends who spend every free weekend on the water between Spain and Morocco.

We named it after Cap Spartel, the lighthouse near Tangier that stands where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean. It is where we fish, and the spirit we want to share.

  1. 01

    Share stories, not coordinates

    We show everything except the waypoint. A spot belongs to the people who found it.

  2. 02

    Respect the sea

    Keep what we eat, release what we don’t, leave every spot as we found it.

  3. 03

    Real fishing only

    No staged catches, no borrowed fish. If it is on Spartel, it happened on our boat.
