Why Spartel

Fishing has always been
a disconnected sport.

Waypoints stuck in a sounder. Catches lost in camera rolls. Conditions forgotten by Monday. The crew’s best spots scattered across WhatsApp messages. Spartel connects all of it, in one app made by anglers.

Connected to your boat

Import and export waypoints and tracks with your Humminbird. Your sounder and your phone finally speak the same language.

Connected to your crew

Shared spots, shared catches, one leaderboard. Everyone on the boat logs into the same story.

Connected to the sea

Wind, swell, water temperature and moon are saved with every fish, so patterns stop being a guess.

Every feature

Everything your boat needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

Spots & map

  • Save every spot with its GPS position, depth, bottom type, notes and sonar screenshot
  • Coordinates in DDM, DMS or decimal, switch in one tap
  • Paste coordinates in any format, use your phone GPS, or drop a pin on the chart
  • Nautical chart with buoys, lights and seamarks, plus a depth layer
  • Search by name or by coordinates, filter by yours, the crew’s or bottom type
  • Each spot shows its catches, top species, best lure and best time of day

Export & import your points

  • Import every waypoint from your Humminbird Solix in one file (GPX)
  • Tracks from the sounder become trips, with distance, duration and top speed
  • Export one spot, or all your spots, as a GPX file
  • Load the file on the Solix, or on any GPS or chartplotter that reads GPX
  • Export a trip’s track to keep it or share it
  • Copy or share a spot’s coordinates with a friend in one tap

Catches

  • Photo and video of every fish
  • Who caught it: tap anyone in the crew, one phone logs for the whole boat
  • Species list for the Atlantic and the Mediterranean
  • Weight, length, depth, technique, released or kept
  • Position from your GPS, a saved spot, the photo itself, or typed in
  • The nearest spot is recognised automatically

Conditions, logged for you

  • Wind speed, direction and gusts at the moment of the catch
  • Swell height and period, water temperature
  • Air pressure, air temperature and cloud cover
  • Moon phase and illumination
  • Live conditions at Cap Spartel on the home screen

Lure box

  • Photograph every lure once: name, brand, type, weight, colour
  • Pick it from your box when you log a catch, or snap a new one on the spot
  • Each lure ranked by catches and species
  • See which lures the whole crew is catching with

Trips

  • Plan the next trip: date, boat, who’s on board
  • Attach the Solix track after the trip
  • Distance, duration and top speed computed from the track
  • Every catch of the trip on the map and in a timeline

Crew

  • Create a crew and invite friends with a link or a code
  • Spots and catches shared with the crew only when you decide
  • Leaderboard: catches and biggest fish per angler
  • Biggest fish of the month on the home screen
  • Several crews if you fish on different boats

Offline & private

  • Works without network at sea: catches wait on the phone and upload back at the port
  • The chart areas you already viewed stay available offline
  • Private by default: nothing is ever public
  • The hidden GPS position inside photos is removed before upload
  • Installs from a link on iPhone and Android, no App Store, free
