Every feature

Everything your boat needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

Spots & map

  • Every spot with its GPS position, depth, bottom type, notes and sonar screenshot
  • Coordinates in DDM, DMS or decimal, switch in one tap
  • Paste coordinates in any format, use your GPS, or drop a pin on the chart
  • Marine chart with depth colours, buoys, lights and seamarks, centred on your favourite base
  • Each spot shows its catches, top species, best lure and best time

Navigate & read the water

  • Guidance to any spot: live distance, bearing and speed
  • Compass mode on the phone, arrow pointing to the spot
  • Heatmap of your friends’ catches on the chart
  • Search by name or by coordinates, filter by yours, your friends’ or bottom type

Export & import your points

  • Import waypoints and tracks from Humminbird, Garmin, Lowrance, Raymarine
  • Export one spot or all of them as GPX, KML or CSV
  • Load them back on your plotter, on any GPS, in Google Earth or a spreadsheet
  • Step-by-step instructions for each sounder brand inside the app
  • Copy or share a spot’s coordinates in one tap

Catches

  • Photo and video of every fish
  • Who caught it: one phone logs for the whole boat
  • Species list for the Atlantic and the Mediterranean
  • Weight, length, depth, technique, released or kept
  • Position from your GPS, a saved spot, the photo itself, or typed in

Conditions, logged for you

  • Wind speed, direction and gusts at the moment of the catch
  • Swell height and period, water temperature
  • Air pressure, air temperature and cloud cover
  • Moon phase and illumination

Forecast & bite times

  • 7-day marine forecast for Cap Spartel, any spot or your position
  • Wind, gusts, swell, water temperature, hour by hour
  • Solunar major and minor periods, sunrise and sunset
  • Modelled tide curve with high and low water
  • Spartel fishing index out of 100 for every day

Pattern finder

  • A profile for every species from your friends’ catches
  • Best lure, technique, time of day, moon and spot
  • The wind, water temperature and depth that work
  • Catches month by month, release rate, records

Lure box

  • Photograph every lure once: name, brand, type, weight, colour
  • Pick it from your box when you log a catch, or snap a new one
  • Each lure ranked by catches and species
  • See which lures your friends are catching with

Trips & live tracking

  • Plan the trip: date, boat, who’s on board
  • Record the track live from the phone, or import it from the sounder
  • Distance, duration and top speed
  • Replay the trip on the map, catch by catch

Friends

  • Invite friends with your personal link: they install the app, sign up, and you are friends instantly
  • Search for anglers by name and send a request
  • Spots and catches shared with your friends only, when you decide
  • Leaderboard: catches and biggest fish among friends
  • Notifications when a friend posts a trip with free seats
  • A public angler profile with the catches you choose, never the spot

Book a trip at sea

  • Join trips posted by friends or by captains, with photos of the boat
  • Request your seats in the app and chat with the captain
  • Pay the captain directly: cash, bank transfer or a deposit, agreed in the chat
  • The captain can share his bank details in the chat in one tap
  • Captains post their boat photos, a description and their price in a minute

Offline & private

  • Works without network at sea: catches and tracks wait on the phone
  • Chart areas you already viewed stay available offline
  • Private by default: only the catches you choose go public, without the spot
  • The hidden GPS position inside photos is removed before upload

Free, no App Store

  • Installs from a link on iPhone and Android
  • Updates arrive instantly, nothing to download
  • No subscription, no ads
  • Your data exports anytime, in open formats

Get started in one minute

Install Spartel on your phone

No App Store, no download: Spartel installs from your browser and opens full screen like any app. It works offline at sea.

Get your link

Request access below, or ask a friend for their personal invite link: you become friends the moment you sign up.

Add it to your home screen

Open the link on your phone and follow the steps for your phone below.

Create your account

First name, email, password, and your favourite fishing base. The map and the weather open centred on it.

iPhone · Safari
  1. Open the link in Safari (from WhatsApp: tap the compass, or “Open in Safari”).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android · Chrome
  1. Open the link in Chrome (from WhatsApp: ⋮ then “Open in Chrome”).
  2. Tap Install the app when Spartel offers it, or the ⋮ menu then Install app.
  3. Spartel appears with your other apps.
